# Unauthenticated DNS Zone Transfer

**Category:** DNS Security

**Status:** Reported

## Summary

An unauthenticated DNS zone transfer issue was identified during security research and reported to the relevant organization.

## Impact

An unrestricted DNS zone transfer may expose DNS records and infrastructure information that should not be publicly available through unauthorized zone transfers.

Potentially exposed information may include:

- Hostnames
- Subdomains
- Internal naming information
- DNS infrastructure records

## Disclosure

The finding was responsibly disclosed to the relevant organization.

No unnecessary access to unrelated systems or user data was performed.

## Public Evidence

Sensitive infrastructure information and unnecessary DNS records are redacted from the public version.
